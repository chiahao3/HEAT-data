01 · What the repository is

Code, inputs and recorded runs for three structure searches

HEAT embeds Effective Atom Theory (EAT) in a genetic algorithm. Each site is a mixture of real species and a null species; positions and mixture weights relax together under a UMA interatomic potential, a Tsallis entropy term (“syntropization”) drives sites to a single species or to null, and the result is rounded and relaxed as ordinary atoms before scoring. Crossover halves both parents’ occupancies (κ = 0.5), superimposes them in the shared cell, prunes overlaps and re-relaxes, so atoms from either parent can survive or vanish. The baseline is a conventional hard-atom GA with cut-and-splice crossover and mutations.

The archive holds the HEAT drivers (3.2k–12k lines each), the conventional GAs, a launcher (run_search.py) with portable configs, the inputs (Mg(BH4)2 CIF, NIST Fe dislocation dumps, the experimental WSe2 phase volume), the recorded logs/metrics of every run in the paper, plotting scripts, and the FAIRChem patch that implements effective atoms. Model weights and per-generation structure exports are not included.

04 · WSe2 structure

HEAT recovers two Se–W–Se trilayers and the twist angle

The paper says the best HEAT structure “resolves the six atomic planes of two twisted Se–W–Se trilayers”. We tested that geometrically on the bundled 280×280 crops, against the independently registered guided reference. Heights are clustered into six planes; a W counts as trilayer-coordinated when it has exactly three Se 1.2–2.2 Å above and three below within 2.9 Å.

Pearson correlation of z-summed projections over the 280×280 display crop: 0.9344 (HEAT) and 0.1241 (conventional) from the bundled TIFFs, 0.9331 and 0.1233 when we re-render from the bundled cropped structures with the production renderer. The experimental crop is reproduced bit-for-bit from the raw TIFF. In both HEAT and the guided reference, adjacent W layers are 7.5–8.0 Å apart, against about 6.5 Å in 2H-WSe2. The layer thickness and W–Se bond length are bulk-like, so the larger spacing likely comes from the experimental depth scale and the 4 Å depth response. The paper does not discuss it.

05 · Effective-atom implementation

The FAIRChem patch does what EAT needs

The archive ships the effective-atom implementation as a patch against FAIRChem cce3699. It applies cleanly. We tested its defining properties on the real uma-s-1p1 model and on FAIRChem’s own random-weight UMA-architecture test checkpoint.

PropertyTestReal UMAStatus

On the random-weight test checkpoint, a null site did shift the energy by 0.01–0.08 eV. That model caps neighbors at 20, and its untrained normalizer is not zero. On the production model (cap 300) the shift disappears. A null site can still occupy one of the 300 neighbor slots when a region is very dense in effective sites; a 6 Å sphere would need more than 0.33 sites/Å3. We did not observe this, but it is a limit of the representation worth stating.

What a fresh reproduction costs

Setup that worked for us: conda Python 3.12, torch 2.8.0+cu126, FAIRChem cce3699 with the bundled patch (editable install), setuptools<81 (torchtnt still imports pkg_resources), access to the gated facebook/UMA weights, and a Materials Project key. Elemental references come from today’s MP entries. W and Se match the archive exactly. For Fe and the Mg/B/H set they differ (see issues). For pure Fe this only shifts every energy by a constant.

09 · Method

How this was checked

  • All computation ran as Slurm jobs on altas, with at most four jobs at once. CPU-only jobs hid the node’s GPUs. No local GPU was used.
  • The UMA checkpoint was downloaded on the workstation and only the model files were copied to the cluster. The Materials Project key was read at run time on altas to fetch six elemental structures into a credential-free JSON. Neither credential is stored in the repository.
  • The drivers were run unmodified. A sitecustomize shim served the fetched MP structures, because the compute jobs get no MP key. Only the generation count, worker count and GPU IDs were overridden. The conventional WSe2 run used transform_epsilon: 1.0e-06.
  • All scripts, the session log and a proposed patch for the launch bugs are in verification/ on branch claude/verify-repo.
